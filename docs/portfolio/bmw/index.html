Industrial Sound Design at BMW Group, Munich
#

Role: Sound Design Intern

Period: January - June 2022

This experience was a turning point that significantly advanced both my professional and personal growth.

My internship at BMW was centered on developing audio tools. Working extensively within a Max/MSP environment, where I implemented real-time, interactive audio features for a multi-speaker system. This involved extending the capabilities of our core Max/MSP abstractions to create a more robust and flexible sound design environment. To streamline the design process itself, I engineered data handling solutions, including an integrated preset system for managing complex sound parameters. A key initiative was developing an automated batch file processor to optimize our audio post-processing workflow, significantly reducing manual tasks.
